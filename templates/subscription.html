Yes! Each week you can select your preferred meals from our rotating menu. We accommodate dietary restrictions and preferences.

No problem! You can pause your subscription for any week through your account dashboard. Just give us 48 hours notice.
Is there a commitment period? ▼
No long-term commitments! You can cancel your subscription anytime with no cancellation fees.
