🔧 API ENDPOINTS

  POST /api/chatbot/message
       Send: {"message": "I want Salmon Power Bowl"}
       Get: {"response": "...", "meal_ordered": true, ...}

  POST /api/chatbot/voice
       Voice transcription handling

  POST /api/chatbot/add-to-cart
       Send: {"meal_id": 1, "quantity": 2}
       Get: {"success": true, "cart": [...]}

  GET /api/chatbot/meals
       Get: [list of all available meals]

❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

  Q: Do I need an API key?
  A: No! Everything works with browser native APIs.

  Q: Is my voice being recorded?
  A: No. Recording only happens when you click the button.

  Q: Does it work on mobile?
  A: Yes! Responsive design works on phones & tablets.

  Q: Which meals does it understand?
  A: All meals in your meals.json file.

  Q: Can it handle typos?
  A: Yes! Uses fuzzy matching (~60% similarity threshold).

  Q: Where are conversations saved?
  A: Not saved (yet). Lost on page reload.

  Q: Is it secure?
  A: Yes. No personal data stored, validated on backend.
